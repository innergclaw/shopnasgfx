Questions and terms

Practical terms for working with me on a SHOPNASGFX project.

General

How do I begin a project?
Submit the brief on the homepage with your name, the service required, and a short description of the work. I review it and reply with the recommended package, the confirmed price, the payment step, and the project setup.
What are the current SHOPNASGFX package prices?
Logo Starter is $100, paid in full. Brand Starter Kit is $250, paid in full. Launch Package is $500, with a 50% deposit. Graphics and agents keep their listed prices on the site. Graphics are paid in full.
What is included in the $100 Logo Starter?
A primary logo and the production-ready files needed to begin use. Full ownership transfers after final delivery.
What is included in the Brand Starter Kit and the Launch Package?
The Brand Starter Kit is $250, paid in full, and includes a main logo, a secondary logo, and an icon/mark, delivered as production-ready files. The Launch Package is $500 and includes everything in the Brand Starter Kit, plus a landing page / website for digital ownership: domain, mailing list, contact, forms, and automation. The Launch Package uses a 50% deposit to start and 50% before final files.
How long does a project take?
Current package timelines range from 5 to 20 business days. I confirm the schedule after I review the scope and have everything required to begin.
Do I own the final designs?
Yes. Full ownership transfers to you on final delivery. There are no watermarks, licensing restrictions, or ongoing fees.
How many revisions do I get?
The Logo Starter includes one revision round. Higher packages include two. Additional rounds start at $50, and I confirm the fee before any further changes are made.
What if I already have an SI-generated design?
I offer SI Design File Fix. I refine the design, correct the layout, and prepare production-ready files for print or digital use.
Can I track my project status?
Yes. Each client receives a project dashboard for progress, updates, previews, and next steps from me and my team. View the tracker
What payment methods do you accept?
I accept Cash App, PayPal, Stripe, and other approved payment methods. Logo Starter, Brand Starter Kit, and graphics are paid in full. The Launch Package uses a 50% deposit to start and 50% before final files. Agents are billed at their listed monthly rate.

Refund Policy

Deposits

The Launch Package requires a 50% non-refundable deposit before I begin, with the remaining 50% due before final files. Logo Starter, Brand Starter Kit, and graphics are paid in full before I begin. Payment reserves the scheduled time and opens project setup.

Before Design Work Begins

If you cancel before any design work has started, I will review any payment beyond the non-refundable deposit. Eligible refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days.

During the Design Process

  • If you cancel after I deliver initial concepts, I may issue a partial refund based on the work completed.
  • Each package includes the stated revision rounds. Describe what needs attention, and I will apply focused changes within that scope.
  • Refunds are not issued for a change of mind after the work has been completed.

After Final Delivery

Once the final files are delivered and approved, the project is complete. I do not issue refunds after final delivery and acceptance.

Business Cards & Printed Materials

Printed business cards are produced for the approved brand. They cannot be cancelled once my team has sent them to print. If the printer confirms a production defect, my team and I will coordinate the reprint.

How to Request a Refund

Email shopnasgfx@gmail.com or text "REFUND" to 267-473-0397 with your name and project details. I respond within 24 hours.
