Questions and terms

Practical terms for working with me on a SHOPNASGFX project.

General

How do I begin a project?
Submit the brief on the homepage with your name, the service required, and a short description of the work. I review it and reply with the recommended package, the confirmed price, the payment step, and the project setup.
What are the current SHOPNASGFX logo and identity prices?
Logo Essentials is $450. Brand Mark, the recommended package, is $1,200. Full Identity is $2,800. Graphics and agents keep their listed prices on the site. Written quotes already issued at a previous price are honored.
What is included in Logo Essentials?
Logo Essentials is $450 and takes 1 to 2 weeks once the brief and deposit are in. It includes a discovery call or written brief, 2 distinct concepts, 2 revision rounds on the chosen direction, a primary logo plus one simple lockup, vector masters in Illustrator (.ai), SVG, EPS, and PDF, a PNG set in full color, black, white, and transparent, and a one-page color and font spec. You get exclusive use of the approved mark and full ownership after final payment.
What is included in Brand Mark and Full Identity?
Brand Mark is $1,200 and takes 2 to 3 weeks. It includes everything in Logo Essentials, then 3 concepts, 3 revision rounds, a logo suite (primary, secondary, and a standalone mark), a color and type system, a mini brand guide, and a social profile set. Full Identity is $2,800 and takes 3 to 4 weeks. It includes everything in Brand Mark, plus a strategy session, 3 to 4 concepts, 3 revision rounds and one polish pass, a full logo system, a brand guide, application mockups agreed at kickoff, and an SI prompt kit trained on your brand rules. Landing pages and site builds are quoted separately.
How long does a project take?
Logo Essentials takes 1 to 2 weeks, Brand Mark takes 2 to 3 weeks, and Full Identity takes 3 to 4 weeks, once the brief and deposit are in. I confirm the schedule after I review the scope.
Do I own the final designs?
Yes. You own the final approved mark and files after final payment, with no watermarks, licensing restrictions, or ongoing fees. Unused concepts stay with ShopNasGfx unless you buy them out. Trademark filing is not included.
How many revisions do I get?
Logo Essentials includes 2 revision rounds. Brand Mark includes 3. Full Identity includes 3 revision rounds plus one polish pass. Extra revision rounds are $150 on Logo Essentials and Brand Mark, and $200 on Full Identity. I confirm the price before making more changes. A new direction after a concept is approved is quoted as new scope, not a revision.
Can you work faster than the listed timeline?
Yes, when the calendar allows. Rush is +25% to pull the timeline in by about a week, or +40% to deliver in about half the listed time. The rush fee is quoted in writing before work starts.
What if I already have an SI-generated design?
I offer SI Design File Fix. I refine the design, correct the layout, and prepare production-ready files for print or digital use.
Can I track my project status?
Yes. Each client receives a project dashboard for progress, updates, previews, and next steps from me and my team. View the tracker
What payment methods do you accept?
I accept Cash App, PayPal, Stripe, and other approved payment methods. Logo and identity packages use a 50% deposit to start and 50% before final files. Projects are not started until the deposit clears. Graphics and other projects under $150 are paid in full.

Refund Policy

Deposits

Logo and identity packages require a 50% non-refundable deposit before I begin, with the remaining 50% due before final files. Graphics and other projects under $150 are paid in full. Payment reserves the scheduled time and opens project setup.

Before Design Work Begins

If you cancel before any design work has started, I will review any payment beyond the non-refundable deposit. Eligible refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days.

During the Design Process

  • If you cancel after I deliver initial concepts, I may issue a partial refund based on the work completed.
  • Each package includes the stated revision rounds. Describe what needs attention, and I will apply focused changes within that scope.
  • Refunds are not issued for a change of mind after the work has been completed.

After Final Delivery

Once the final files are delivered and approved, the project is complete. I do not issue refunds after final delivery and acceptance.

Business Cards & Printed Materials

Printed business cards are produced for the approved brand. They cannot be cancelled once my team has sent them to print. If the printer confirms a production defect, my team and I will coordinate the reprint.

How to Request a Refund

Email shopnasgfx@gmail.com or text "REFUND" to 267-473-0397 with your name and project details. I respond within 24 hours.
